# Risk Register

This document tracks project risks identified during meetings and planning sessions. Entries include the risk description, potential impact, likelihood, mitigation strategy, owner, and status.

| ID | Risk Description | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|----|------------------|--------|------------|------------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Team members lack oncology/urology expertise | Misinterpretation of clinical patterns could lead to misleading insights | High | Consult domain experts, validate findings with literature | Team / PM | Open |
| 2 | Symptoms definition too broad (e.g. "lower-abdomen pain") | May introduce noise and false positives in analyses | Medium | Refine symptom list, document assumptions, iterate with clinical input | Analysts | Open |
| 3 | Dataset limited to two hospitals | Results may not generalize; risk of overfitting patterns | High | Note limitations clearly in reports; seek external data if feasible later | PM/Team | Open |
| 4 | Potential misdiagnosis patterns leading to legal risk | Incorrect conclusions could expose stakeholder to lawsuits | High | Focus on descriptive analysis, avoid causal claims; include disclaimers | PM/Team | Open |
| 5 | Time/budget constraints for retraining staff | Solution recommendations may not be implementable | Medium | Manage stakeholder expectations; prioritize high-impact actionable insights | PM | Open |
